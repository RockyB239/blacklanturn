BLACKLANTURN

Browsing with a remote
Press Left from the first card to expand the sidebar. Use Up/Down to choose a destination and OK to open it. Press Right to return to the cards. Back returns through the current page or closes a dialog. Highlight a title, then press C, the remote context-menu key or right-click for its actions. Previous and Next cards move between provider pages; returning restores the prior page and selection.

Movies, shows and genres
In Movies or Shows, use Options → Choose genre… to filter discovery rows. Cancel keeps the current page and title. Regular discovery rows exclude anime identified by animation and Japanese origin metadata; missing metadata alone does not hide a title. Animation & Anime, direct searches, saved titles, watchlists and Continue Watching retain intentional access. A discovery page fills up to 20 eligible titles when more are available; the final page can be shorter.

Search
Choose Search at the top of the sidebar and enter a title, person, studio or collection. All combines the supported categories; use Movies, Shows, People, Studios or Collections to narrow results. Person cards open filmographies; studio and collection cards open their titles. Back returns to your results and selected card. Search is supplied by TMDbHelper and does not search every installed add-on. The last term is saved on this device. Settings → Help → Clear saved search removes it.

Details, episodes and playback
Details shows the selected title, description and available actions. Movie and show details can include cast and similar titles. Details → All episodes opens seasons, including Specials when supplied. Episode cards retain their numbers and titles. Resume uses the selected episode's saved position when supported by the playback integration. Stopping playback returns to your browsing location.

Next episode
Umbrella supplies the continuation popup. Play now uses Umbrella's next-episode action. Stop watching stops playback and clears its queue. Dismiss or Back closes the popup without cancelling the next episode. Countdown expiry follows Umbrella's selected action. Settings → Accounts & integrations → Playback & provider accounts opens Umbrella; its Playback / PlayNext options control timing and the default action. Other playback integrations may supply their own popup.

My Stuff and Trakt
My Stuff saves bookmarks on this device. Trakt watchlists, watched history and movie/show progress depend on your configured integration and Trakt account. Use title Options → Trakt · Watched, progress & lists for available Trakt actions. Options → Refresh watched status reads updated status after changes elsewhere. A progress entry does not by itself confirm its position is accurate or that playback will resume successfully. Failed refreshes retain the last known status; avoid repeated retries when a service is rate-limited.

YouTube
Connect your account in Settings → Accounts & integrations → YouTube account & previews. When connected, the YouTube sidebar section offers Home, Search, Subscriptions, Library and Channels. Open channels and playlists to browse their videos. Selecting a video plays it; Details shows its title, channel, description and supported actions. Back returns to browsing. Home's Refresh recommendations action requests an updated feed.
YouTube watched markers and resume information come from the YouTube add-on's local playback history. They are separate from Trakt movie/show progress and may differ from the YouTube website. Saving a YouTube video to My Stuff creates a bookmark on this device. Full playback uses the original YouTube route; previews suppress watch-history writes.

Previews
Settings → Previews → Movie & show automatic previews switches movie/show browsing between Automatic and Manual. YouTube automatic previews is a separate switch for highlighted YouTube videos; changing one leaves the other preference intact. Preview delay controls how long you stay on a card before automatic playback. In Manual mode, use the hero Preview button. Details also offers a manual Preview action when available. Moving away or opening another page cancels the current preview.
Movie/show previews require the YouTube add-on to be enabled with its personal API configuration present. Configure accounts and API values in the add-on's settings. The setup check does not prove account validity or that every trailer is available. Connected YouTube video previews use the selected video's own route. Failed movie/show preview candidates try alternatives when available; artwork remains if none works. Preview title checks reject explicit title mismatches but do not guarantee spoken language, publisher authenticity or a particular season.

Ratings and appearance
Settings → Ratings → Rating sources chooses the available scores; Rating order moves enabled scores left or right. Scores appear when supplied for the selected movie or show. Settings → Appearance controls title logos, time, date and weather. Weather uses your configured Kodi service and location; a missing reading does not block browsing.

Accounts and unavailable lists
Settings → Accounts & integrations opens TMDbHelper for Discovery, Trakt & metadata, Umbrella for Playback & provider accounts, and YouTube for its account and previews. Use each add-on's supported setup screens. Integration status reports installed/enabled versions and failed lists; it does not verify account authorization or subscriptions.
An empty list returned no titles. An unavailable list failed to load. Use Options → Retry unavailable lists for one retry, then check the same list in its add-on or open integration settings. Loading placeholders are not selectable media. All Kodi controls remain available through Settings → Kodi settings.

Playback preferences and recovery
Kodi's Player settings control audio and subtitle preferences. Language selection depends on the flags supplied by each stream. Software-only updates preserve existing profile preferences unless a correction was specifically requested.
Settings → Help → Return to previous skin switches to the saved skin. If this page cannot open, use the skin's Home screen → Kodi settings or Return to previous skin. Kodi Settings → Interface → Skin also allows choosing another installed skin. Use these recovery controls for a skin issue; Fresh Install erases profile data.
