BlackLanturn 1.1.9 - Kodi 21.3 Omega

Update BlackLanturn Installer to 1.0.15 through the BlackLanturn Repository, then select General or Apple TV & Mac in Builds. Kodi installation/signing is separate.

YouTube in BlackLanturn
Controller 0.1.76 retains Home, Search, Subscriptions, Library and Channels inside the existing skin. Results use the YouTube add-on's currently selected signed-in account. YouTube appears in the sidebar when that account has a saved refreshable authorization and disappears on sign-out, account removal or add-on disablement. Temporary account-read failures retain the last known connection during that window session. Account changes invalidate cached private rows, including playback-return snapshots. No personal account is included in either download. Connect your own account in Settings > Accounts > YouTube account & previews.

Highlighting a playable video starts that exact video in the existing preview area after your configured Preview Delay. The preview is limited to 30 seconds, suppresses local/remote watch-history reporting and suggested-video enqueue, and stops when selection changes. Settings > Previews > YouTube automatic previews controls this independently of movie/TV preview mode. Play opens the original provider URL with its normal playback/history/resume behavior. Library respects remote/custom playlist choices and explicit local-only Watch Later/History choices. Unavailable, restricted or unplayable videos can still fail at the YouTube service; an authorization is not proof of live service availability.

Preserving an existing setup
Before installing, retain a private recovery copy. Under Installer Settings > Install Settings leave "Clean install: keep only selected accounts" off to retain preferences. Enable the desired Keep options, including Keep My guisettings.xml for device playback settings, Keep My AdvancedSettings.xml and Keep My Trakt/Debrid/Login Data. Standard updates retain the existing explicit playback/Trakt preference policy and selected authorizations. Account-only clean mode intentionally resets other preferences to build defaults. Native playback components remain device-specific. Use the existing 1.1.7 setup guide for the detailed preservation policy and tvOS cleanup limits.

Both editions retain schema 3, playback-trakt-1 and the existing Installer 1.0.13 policy minimum; 1.0.15 advances the catalog/package version, with no changes to cleanup/account logic. The reviewed Helper 6.17.9 empty-cache-1/search-batch-1 tree and its update protection remain unchanged. Playback follows Kodi's interface language with forced-only subtitles on a new install. Cocoscrapers, automatic stream selection, High artwork and 64MB/fixed400/buffermode4/chunk131072 buffer defaults remain included. Existing device HDR, Dolby Vision and decoder choices must be preserved through the chosen update settings; Android decorative previews temporarily use software decoding to prevent startup/cancellation deadlocks. Both original decoder preferences are journaled and restored before normal videos and after cancellation/restart; this does not replace the device playback defaults.

Validation and limits
The exact controller passed native Mac Kodi 21.3 acceptance: 50 recommendations and 50 subscription video IDs matched the signed-in add-on; sidebar connected/disconnected/reconnected/transient handling, all tabs, search, channel navigation/back, account-scoped return, independent preview disablement and exact-video embedded preview/cancellation passed. Disconnection used an in-memory diagnostic reader, without editing or revoking the real account. Local watch-history contents stayed unchanged, the player reported both history channels disabled, and settings/authorization grants matched private recovery. Fire Stick controller76 also passed cancellation while resolving, opening and playing, normal H264 hardware-decoder restoration, four browsing rounds, six searches, two YouTube cycles and an ordinary restart. Thirteen protected settings/account files and local YouTube history matched. This is not independent remote-history API verification. Apple TV was not accessed, tested, restarted or updated during this release; existing Apple TV playback is untouched. Full Play's original URL preservation is covered by controller regression tests; a watched full video was not used for acceptance.

Release tooling verifies the exact native-tested controller manifest in both clean packages, the wrapped installer, all managed Helper files and protected playback defaults. Builds originate from immutable clean baselines, never a live profile. Existing release assets and private recovery remain available for rollback.
