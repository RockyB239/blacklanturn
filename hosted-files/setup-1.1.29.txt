BlackLanturn Build 1.1.29 includes Installer 1.0.34 in both General and Apple TV/Mac packages.

Updates now uses the newest known release for your installed edition instead of an older saved manual check. Update BlackLanturn is selected when entering Updates if an update is available; returning retains your selection. Opening the page adds no network requests.

Use Update BlackLanturn for the in-place update. Bundled add-ons, Installer and reviewed patches update together while settings, authorizations, playback preferences, history, sources and other add-ons stay in place. Close and reopen Kodi after updating.

Controller0.1.95 and playback code are unchanged. Validation uses isolated synthetic profiles; no personal device or notice history was changed.
