BlackLanturn Build 1.1.26 includes Installer 1.0.31 and Controller 0.1.93 in both General and Apple TV/Mac editions.

- Dedicated Updates sidebar, scrollable release notes, distinct Updates/Maintenance icons and clearer update-versus-reinstall wording.
- Choose genre restored in main Movies/Shows title options, including lower rows.
- Recommendations-first More like this, Similar fallback and independent branded Next/Previous navigation.
- Broader valid trailer labels plus one limited, cached missing-preview lookup with wrong-series and conflicting-year checks. Previews remain excluded from YouTube watch history.
- Existing managed add-on versions, tested patches, platform playback workarounds and clean defaults retained.

Update BlackLanturn preserves authorizations, playback preferences, watched/resume/history data, sources, favourites and unrelated add-ons. Update Installer through the repository, then use Updates → Check now → Update BlackLanturn. Installer 1.0.30 remains the supported update-format minimum; Installer 1.0.31 is bundled and is the recommended interface. Standard/Fresh are separate installation modes. Close and reopen Kodi after updating.

Available-update notices repeat each Kodi launch unless explicitly hidden for that release; future releases remain eligible. Personal devices have not been updated or their real release notices acknowledged during publication, so users can observe the normal startup notification.

Release acceptance is bound to exact immutable packages: clean, Standard, in-place preservation, native Kodi21.3 and production startup-notification checks. General platform identity in macOS native tests is simulated; this does not claim fresh physical Fire Stick/tvOS playback acceptance.
