BlackLanturn Build 1.1.27 includes Installer 1.0.32 and Controller 0.1.94 in both General and Apple TV/Mac editions.

- The protected in-place transaction now updates the Installer together with bundled add-ons and BlackLanturn patches. Newer installed Installer versions are kept.
- Installer replacement occurs last, and interrupted updates restore it first. A separate controller startup entrypoint can recover it even if its folder is missing.
- The outgoing Installer navigation closes after updating, avoiding old code continuing against new files.
- Settings, authorizations, playback preferences, watched/resume/history data, sources, favourites and unrelated add-ons stay in place. Existing platform workarounds remain included.

Existing Installer 1.0.31 and older need to receive 1.0.32 through Kodi's normal repository update once. After Build 1.1.27 and Installer 1.0.32 are installed, Update BlackLanturn includes future Installer versions without a separate manual Installer step. Close and reopen Kodi after updating.

Both editions are tested with isolated Kodi21.3 install/update and startup recovery checks. Android identity in macOS acceptance is simulated; this does not claim new physical Fire Stick or tvOS testing. Personal devices and real notification acknowledgements are left untouched so the user can observe the published update notice.
